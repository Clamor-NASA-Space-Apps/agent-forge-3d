# Third-party skills in this folder

Eighteen of the skills here are not ours. They are copied unchanged from
[awesome-gamedev-agent-skills](https://github.com/gamedev-skills/awesome-gamedev-agent-skills)
(commit `dcf3bbbb83f365632402c086bf595048aa24aaa8`, 10 November 2026), which is licensed under
Apache-2.0. Its licence and notice are in `third-party/gamedev-skills.LICENSE` and
`third-party/gamedev-skills.NOTICE`.

| Copied skill | Covers |
|---|---|
| `godot-gdscript`, `godot-nodes-scenes`, `godot-signals-groups`, `godot-resources` | the language, scenes, signals, data resources |
| `godot-3d-essentials`, `godot-physics`, `godot-animation`, `godot-shaders`, `godot-audio` | 3D scenes, bodies and queries, clips and trees, shaders, sound |
| `godot-ui-control`, `godot-export`, `godot-gdscript-headless-testing` | HUD, builds, tests without the editor |
| `camera-systems`, `input-systems`, `performance-optimization`, `game-ai` | engine-neutral techniques |
| `survival-crafting`, `game-jam` | a genre and a deadline workflow |

Rules for these folders:
- Never edit them. To update, copy the newer version over and change the commit above.
- This repo's code rules (no em dashes and so on) apply to our own skills, not to these.
- Where one of them disagrees with `AGENTS.md` or with one of our skills, ours wins: ours carry
  what was measured on real builds.

Our own skills are `character-body`, `character-life`, `contextual-traversal`, `game-feel-gate`,
`living-world`, `reality-sheet`, `third-person-camera` and `world-collision`.
